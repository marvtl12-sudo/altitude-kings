Our story

Built by mountains.
Guided by experience.

Altitude Kings was born from a deep love for Kashmir's mountains and the belief that the best journeys are the ones that bring you closer to a place—and to yourself.

Founded by Owais, we are a Kashmir-based team of local mountain lovers, outdoor professionals and thoughtful hosts. We know these trails because they are part of our home. Every trip is planned with care, led with experience and shaped around the people who join us.
